• MOTHER AND CHILD CARE
  • Breast Feeding Week Promotion
    Breast Feeding Week Promotion is one of the major activities of IDEAH since its inception. Educating on the Importance of breast feeding and age appropriate feeding are part of its regular community meetings.
  • Mothers Meetings
    Mothers Meetings are regular activities to Educate on pre natal, anti natal and post natal issues. Importance of immunization for both mother and child, need for periodical health checkups, institutional deliveries.
  • Saamuhika Seemanthalu, Uyyala, and Anna Prasana
    Saamuhika Seemanthalu, Uyyala, and Anna Prasana to promote mother and child health and promote importance and sc ientific aspects of our traditional systems.
  • Health Baby Meets
    Health Baby Meets are annually organized to identify healthy children between the age group of 6 months to 2 years and awarded “Health Baby” Title. Local ICDS workers are involved in this activity.
  • Women’s Day Celebrations
    Women’s Day Celebrations intended to educate and empower women about their rights and to guide them about women protection acts and taking legal support in needy situations.
  • World Child Rights Week
    World Child Rights Week An annual activity of the organization. Rallies with the students, meetings with the teachers and parents are organized on this event. Information related to Child Rights and Juvenile Justice was also shared.
  • Adolescent Week Celebrations
    Adolescent Week Celebrations the adolescents were educated on age specific growth and changes / problems
