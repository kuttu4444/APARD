• SCHOOL & LITERACY
  • Badi Bata (“Back to School”)
    Badi Bata (“Back to School”) is to ensure enrolment of school-age children into the local schools. Rallies, and meetings are organized by involving the students, parents, teachers, elected representatives of the village etc. Local anganwadi (ICDS) workers also played key role by providing information about the children who have completed 6 years and to be admitted into the schools.
  • Campaign against Child Labour
    Campaign against Child Labour is another important activity to educate the communities about the importance of schooling and give the child its “Right to Education”.
  • Saamuhika Seemanthalu, Uyyala, and Anna Prasana
    Saamuhika Seemanthalu, Uyyala, and Anna Prasana to promote mother and child health and promote importance and sc ientific aspects of our traditional systems.
  • Teacher-Parents Meetings
    Teacher-Parents Meetings are organized to educate and motivate parents to send their children to school. Distributed Books, Plats and Glasses for the students to provide them with the basic comforts they require.
  • Sports and Games Competitions
    Sports and Games Competitions for students in the school to promote competitive spirit and to bring out the hidden talents in them.
  • Campaign against Violence
    Campaign against Violence at Schools is also a major activity of IDEAH to promote “No Stick” schools and “Child Friendly Schools.”
  • Awareness on ChildLine 1098
    Awareness on ChildLine 1098 to help the children understand their safety and call for support in trauma.
